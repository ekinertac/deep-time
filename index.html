#EraYou lastThe limiting factor

Three rules that decide everything

01 — Oxygen is the first filter

You need roughly 16% atmospheric oxygen to stay conscious. Earth did not reliably have that until about 430 million years ago. Everything before that is a suffocation problem and nothing else matters.

02 — Then food is the filter

Breathable air arrives long before edible land. No grass until roughly 55 Ma, no fruit until the mid-Cretaceous. A diet of lean meat alone becomes fatal in weeks once protein passes about 35% of calories.

03 — Disease is a non-issue

Pathogens co-evolve with their hosts. Nothing in the Jurassic has any idea what to do with your immune system. What still kills you is an ordinary infected wound, in every era, with no antibiotics anywhere.
