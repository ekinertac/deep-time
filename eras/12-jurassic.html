Interval 12 / 16 Mesozoic

Jurassic

201.4 – 145 million years ago  ·  56 Myr long  ·  1.2% of Earth's history

The one everybody pictures, and one of the worse places on this list to actually be. Warm, humid, ice-free and covered in vegetation, none of which you can digest. It is a garden with no staple in it, patrolled by animals with no concept of what you are.

You last ~2 months

The same figure as the Silurian, 220 million years later, for the same reason: no digestible plants. The Jurassic gives you excellent shelter material, reliable water and a pleasant climate, and then hands you a menu of conifer needles and toxic seeds.

Conditions

Atmospheric oxygen ~13–20%est.

Scale 0–40%. Recovering from the Triassic minimum. Somewhere between comfortable and mildly thin, depending on which model you trust.

Edible plants no staplenone reliable

No flowering plants at all, so no fruit, no grain, no grass, no leafy greens, no tubers. Gymnosperm seeds and some fern shoots are edible in principle, and this page notes ginkgo seeds in small quantity, but each needs species knowledge and preparation you would not have. What is missing is a staple, not every last calorie.

Carbon dioxide ~1,000–2,000ppm

Scale 0–4,000 ppm. Enough to keep the poles ice-free and the mid-latitudes humid year round.

Mean global temp ~21 °C

Scale −20 to 250 °C. Roughly 6 °C above modern, and mild almost everywhere. As climates on this site go, the Jurassic is genuinely pleasant.

Day length ~23hours

Scale 0–24 h. About 380 days per year.

Largest animal ~26 msauropod

Scale 0–30 m. Herbivores, and no threat by intent. A herd of forty-tonne animals moving through the same clearing you are in is a hazard for reasons that have nothing to do with aggression.

What kills you

Hour 1

It looks like the best place yet

Your habitat instincts were tuned on flowering-plant landscapes, where green reliably means fruit, leaf, tuber or seed somewhere within reach. The Jurassic breaks that link. It holds more plant matter than almost any interval on this site and close to none of it converts into calories you can absorb, so the misreading takes weeks to make itself felt.

Day 1

Nothing looks at you with recognition

Not fear, not hunger, not aggression. Nothing in the Jurassic has a category for a two-metre bipedal mammal. Large animals investigate unfamiliar objects at close range, and at eight metres and two tonnes, investigation is enough.

Worth setting straight, since the name misleads: the age of dinosaurs is the whole Mesozoic, not this period. They appear in the Triassic around 230 Ma and run to 66 Ma, and the Cretaceous that follows has both the longest span and the most variety. Stegosaurus, here, is separated from Tyrannosaurus by 84 million years, more time than separates Tyrannosaurus from you.

Week 1

You cannot eat the forest

Conifer needles are indigestible. Cycad seeds are hepatotoxic and neurotoxic. Ferns contain thiaminase, which destroys vitamin B1, plus carcinogens. Ginkgo seeds are edible in small quantity and toxic in large. There is no safe staple anywhere in the Jurassic flora.

Week 4

Meat only, again

Fish, small mammals, lizards, insects, eggs, and the small feathered dinosaurs themselves, crow-sized theropods are as catchable as anything else here. Dinosaur eggs are the best calories on the planet and are guarded by animals that outweigh you by two orders of magnitude.

Month 2

Deficiency, or one bad encounter

Same failure mode as the Silurian. All-protein intake, no vitamin C, no carbohydrate. The Jurassic is a beautiful place to slowly starve in.

If you survive

Live on the water. Rivers and coasts are the only reliable larder.

Air

Fine. Somewhere between modern and slightly thin.

Water

Abundant and clean. Humid climate, permanent rivers, no drought season in most latitudes.

Food

Animal protein only, and nothing keeps. Every day starts by hunting again, because the one storable thing in the landscape is a seed that would poison you.

Fire

Excellent. Conifer wood is resinous and burns hot.

Shelter

The best on this site so far. Straight conifer timber, and enough of it to build properly.

Threats

Genuine. Allosaurus, Ceratosaurus and Torvosaurus all hunt animals your size, and none of them are shy.

Where the numbers are soft

How dinosaurs would treat a human is unknowable. Everything on this page about behaviour is inference from body plan and from how large modern animals respond to unfamiliar objects. It should be read as reasoning, not as fact.
Plant toxicity is extrapolated from living relatives. Modern cycads, ferns and ginkgo carry the compounds described here. Assuming Jurassic members of these groups had similar chemistry is reasonable (plant defence compounds are often ancient and conserved), but it is an assumption.
Jurassic oxygen has a wide model spread. The range here spans "noticeably thin" to "modern", and no proxy resolves it cleanly. This uncertainty does not change the verdict, because food, not air, is what limits you.
