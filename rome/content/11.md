# The Normans burn the part you live in

On 28 May 1084 Robert Guiscard’s Normans took Rome to free Gregory VII from the emperor and burned a broad band of the inhabited city between the Caelian and the Colosseum. Captives were carried off and sold. Structurally it did more damage than either 5th-century sack.

This is the century where the pilgrim regime is at its most dependable and the city itself is at its least. The institutions want you. The buildings are on fire.
