# A man who cannot name his people

The largest city on earth, and a large share of its labour is owned outright. You arrive filthy, speaking nothing anyone recognises, unable to name a father, a tribe, a patron or a home town, wearing clothes of a cut no one has seen and carrying a knife you cannot explain.

Roman law had no gentle reading of that. Free status was a thing you proved, through witnesses, a patron, a birth registration. Someone held as a slave carried the burden of proving otherwise, and in classical law could not even plead their own freedom suit: a free third party, the *adsertor libertatis*, had to stand up and do it for them. You do not know one person in this city.
