# Everybody now has a nationality. You do not.

Before unification you needed a passport simply to travel from Milan to Rome, between Italian states. Then the artillery breaches the Aurelian Walls at the Porta Pia on 20 September 1870, Rome becomes the capital of Italy, and the modern apparatus lands on it: the first national census in 1871, and in the same year a law making the *anagrafe*, the municipal population register, compulsory in every commune in the country.

This is the trap in its final form. Every earlier Rome had a slot for a man who could not account for himself, whether that slot was a slave market or a pilgrim bed. This one has no slot at all. There is no country to return you to, because no country will admit that you exist.
