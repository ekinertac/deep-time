# The peak, which is the worst possible arrival

Everything works. Eleven aqueducts running, a grain fleet from Egypt and Africa, free baths, free games, a standard of urban living Europe will not see again for seventeen hundred years.

None of it is addressed to you. Each of those systems is keyed to a status you cannot demonstrate: the grain dole went to named men on a list. Around forty-five inscribed slave collars survive from the later empire, and one of them, the Zoninus collar, still carries its tag: *Fugi, tene me. Cum revocaveris me domino meo Zonino, accipis solidum.* I have run away, hold me; return me to my master Zoninus and you get a gold coin. That is the object the city has ready for a man who cannot account for himself.
