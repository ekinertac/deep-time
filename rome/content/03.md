# The century the city admits it is afraid

Civil wars, coups, a currency that stops meaning anything, and in 271 Aurelian starts building a wall around a city that had not needed one for five hundred years. It is finished around 275: roughly nineteen kilometres of circuit, enclosing about 13.7 square kilometres, brick-faced concrete, eight metres high before Maxentius and Honorius doubled it.

It is still there. Those walls remained Rome’s actual military defence until Italian artillery breached them at the Porta Pia on 20 September 1870, roughly sixteen hundred years in service. Everything that follows on this page happens inside them.
