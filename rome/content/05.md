# Sacked twice, and still the largest thing anyone has seen

Alaric takes the city in 410 after three sieges; the Vandals take it over two weeks in 455. Neither ends Rome. What drains it is slower and duller: the court had already moved to Ravenna, and when the Vandals took Carthage in 439 the African grain that made a million people possible in a place that could not feed them stopped arriving.

The city is not destroyed. It is defunded. People leave because the free bread that was the entire economic basis of living here has gone.
