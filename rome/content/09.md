# Raided, walled again, still open to you

In August 846 Arab raiders came up the Tiber and sacked St Peter’s and St Paul’s, which were vulnerable for one specific reason: both stood outside the Aurelian Walls. Leo IV answered by walling the Vatican. The Leonine City, finished around 852, is the first expansion of Rome’s defences in six hundred years, and it is the walled Borgo that every later pilgrim walks into.

The city is poor, small and frequently ungoverned. None of that is your problem. Your problem was ever only whether there is a category here for a man who arrives on foot with nothing, and the category is the town’s entire economy.
