# The richest Rome, and the one with no way in

Antibiotics, surgery, clean water, hospitals that will treat anyone, and no chance at all. This city runs on documents: residence permits, work papers, ration cards, and from 1931 a compulsory national identity card. The city also finally bursts its 3rd-century wall and spreads across the Campagna.

You would not starve here. Somebody would find you within days. Then a system with no category for you would spend the rest of your life deciding which of its wrong boxes to file you in.
