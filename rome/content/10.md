# Cattle in the Forum

The centre of the ancient world is grazing land. The Forum is used as pasture and cattle market for so long that its ordinary working name becomes the **Campo Vaccino**, the cow field. The name is securely attested from the 16th century, Claude Lorrain painted it as pasture in 1636, and it was still a cow field when the first excavations began in 1803.

The ground is rising as it goes. Demolition debris, Tiber silt and ordinary rubbish lift the low-lying centre by up to about nine metres over two thousand years. At the Pantheon you once climbed steps up to the porch; now you walk down into it.
