# Rome builds a quarter for foreigners who cannot explain themselves

The *Schola Saxonum*, the English quarter in the Borgo, is traditionally founded in 727 by Ine of Wessex, a king who abdicated in order to walk to Rome and die there. The date rests on later chroniclers, but the institution is real and it sat alongside Frankish, Frisian and Lombard *scholae*: national compounds with churches, burial grounds and lodging for arrivals who spoke no Latin.

Read that against your own position. A man walks in, cannot speak, has no papers, knows nobody, and there is a building for him, run by people whose entire job is this. Not tolerated. Expected, and budgeted for. The district is still called Sassia, and in 1201 Innocent III turned the site into the Ospedale di Santo Spirito, which is a working hospital today.
