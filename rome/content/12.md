# A commune, a guidebook, and a labour market

Rome revives a commune in 1144, and by mid-century there is a written guide for visitors, the *Mirabilia Urbis Romae*, explaining the ruins to people who have walked several months to look at them. Pilgrimage now has a service sector: lodging, food, guiding, money-changing, portering, laundry, burial.

That sector runs on unattached arrivals with strong backs. Here your lack of family, guild, letter or local claim is not a suspicious absence. It is the standard profile of the workforce.
