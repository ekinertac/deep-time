# The state learns to write people down

On 6 May 1527 mutinous imperial troops storm the city and hold it for months. Six to ten thousand are killed; most of the population loss is flight, then plague and famine. That is the visible catastrophe.

The invisible one matters more to you. The Roman Inquisition is re-established in 1542 and takes an active interest in foreigners. From the early 17th century every parish compiles a *stato delle anime*, a name-by-name census of every soul in every household, street by street, once a year at Easter. A hospice asks whether you are hungry. A register asks who you are, and writes down the answer.
