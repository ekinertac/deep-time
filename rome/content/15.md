# The last century in which nobody asks

The building sites open. Rome becomes a construction economy, quarrying its own ruins for lime and travertine, raising new churches and eventually a new St Peter’s, importing unskilled labour by the thousand.

For an undocumented arrival this is the high point of the whole two thousand years: work that asks no questions, a hospice for every nation, charity as institutional routine, and the apparatus that will later demand to know who you are has not been built yet.
